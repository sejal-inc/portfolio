About

Hi, I’m Sejal.

I’m a marketing designer and brand strategist based in Mumbai. I trained in visual communication at UID Gandhinagar, and started working in my second year of college. Since then I’ve designed for studios, a fast-growing SaaS company and D2C brands.

Most of my work sits where design meets marketing: blogs, product pages, reports and campaigns that need to be understood quickly and remembered for longer. I start with the reader, what they already know, what they need, and what would make them trust a brand, and design back from there.

Good design is a kind of empathy: it does the hard work so the reader doesn’t have to.
