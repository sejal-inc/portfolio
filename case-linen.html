The problem

A website move had quietly cut the brand off from Google

The brand makes block-printed cotton table linen that wipes clean and doesn’t slide. In early 2025 it moved its website to a new platform. Sales from search fell to a fraction of what they had been, and the paid team started buying ads on the brand’s own name.

We were hired to oversee SEO. At that point, nobody knew why search had dropped.

What we found

Five problems, each hiding the next

Dead links20 of 21 old links tested led to “page not found”
The wrong reportThe team checked a report showing 0 pages, against 448 in the right one
Broken trackingOnly 3.5% of sales were recorded
Unreadable pages60 tablecloths shared one title; 49 pages were missing from Google
A false alarmAn audit said AI tools were blocked. We tested it: they weren’t

We tested every claim before acting, and matched analytics, the store and the payment gateway until revenue agreed within 0.72%.

The brand had a point of view. The website didn’t show it.

The brand online

Beautiful and practical, buried in a bullet list

The founder couldn’t find table linen that was both beautiful and practical, so she made her own. A first-time visitor saw none of that: the site read like three different writers, warm photography sat in a plain template, and the main promise, wipeable and anti-slip, was buried.

What we built

The brand’s personality, as tools the team can use

A working session with the founder gave us one principle, functional yet beautiful, and a core customer: someone who hosts, and wants a home that looks good but is lived in.

DescriptionsMood, where it fits, one pairing, the practical promise last. 49 rewritten.
Claim badgesWipeable, anti-slip, 100% cotton: the brand’s own promise.
NamingEvery print evokes a place, ingredient, colour or memory.
Product pagesIcons for key features, one link to the size guide, short sections.

One page, before and after

From 300 crowded words to 90 clear ones

TitleBefore: about 130 characters of keywords. Now: print, product, colour, brand.
OpeningBefore: seven products to pair it with. Now: the mood of the print and the home it suits.
Honest limitsBefore: “not stain resistant” listed as a feature. Now: answered properly in the FAQs.
