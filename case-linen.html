A website move had quietly cut the brand off from Google

The brand makes block-printed cotton table linen that wipes clean and doesn’t slide. In early 2025 it moved its website to a new platform. Sales from search fell to a fraction of what they had been, and the paid team started buying ads on the brand’s own name to reach people already looking for it.

We were hired to oversee SEO. At that point, nobody knew why search had dropped.

Five problems, each hiding the next

ProblemIn numbers
Dead links20 of 21 old links tested led to “page not found”
The wrong reportThe team was checking a Google report showing 0 pages, against 448 in the right one
Broken trackingOnly 3.5% of sales were recorded
Unreadable pages60 tablecloths shared one title; 49 real pages were missing from Google
A false alarmAn audit said AI tools were blocked. We tested it: they weren’t

We tested every claim before acting, and matched analytics, the store and the payment gateway until revenue agreed within 0.72%.

The brand had a point of view. The website didn’t show it.

The founder couldn’t find table linen that was both beautiful and practical, so she made her own. A first-time visitor saw none of that: the site read like three different writers, the warm photography sat in a plain white template, and the brand’s main promise, wipeable and anti-slip, was buried in a bullet list.

We turned the brand’s personality into tools the team can use

A working session with the founder and a questionnaire gave us one working principle, functional yet beautiful, and a core customer: someone who hosts, and wants a home that looks good but is lived in.

Description frameworkFour parts in four to six sentences: mood, where it fits, one pairing, the practical promise last. 49 descriptions rewritten.
Claim badgesWipeable, anti-slip, 100% cotton: the brand’s own promise, instead of chasing a certificate.
Naming checklistEvery print evokes a place, landscape, ingredient, colour or memory, now used for new categories too.
Product page layoutIcons for key features, one link to the size guide, repeated details in short sections.
